Chestnut Pergola

Originally to be made of old hop poles this garden pergola was constructed in newly coppiced chestnut from local woods. It will support Wisteria, Clematis and other plants and was intended to attract more birds into the garden.



Following a curved path to a new garden gate



Testing the geometry and the connections.



The posts are charred to resist rot where they will be buried.



The timbers are bolted together in three directions to make stiff connections and avoid cross bracing.



Coming together.
